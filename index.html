FAQ

Common questions

What does “low voltage” mean?

It covers the wiring and systems that run on low power: security cameras, intercoms, access control, alarms, network and phone cabling, Wi-Fi, TV and sound. Not regular electrical wiring.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We come look at the property, talk through what you need and give you a quote. No obligation.

Can I see my cameras on my phone?

Yes. Camera systems we install can be set up for live view and playback on your phone.

Can you work with the system I already have?

Often, yes. Tell us what is there now and we will check what can be kept, upgraded or replaced.

Do you work on large buildings and new construction?

Yes — from a single home to multi-unit buildings, offices, shuls and schools, including wiring for new builds and renovations.

How do I get a price?
